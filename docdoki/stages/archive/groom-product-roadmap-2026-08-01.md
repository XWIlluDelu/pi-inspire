# Product roadmap

## Objective

Order the work needed to establish the first complete local daily-use baseline and its initial detachable connection module.

## Current state

- Completed: session continuity, Git and conversation inspection, daily-use accelerators, branching, bounded history, evidence-gated maintenance, release packaging, and correctness boundaries form the `v0.1.0` local baseline.
- Completed: the detachable `ssh-reverse` sample and generic forwarded-HTTPS hardening preserve the loopback host as Pi and data authority.
- Closed: no product expansion is selected through this roadmap; independently grounded work now uses a focused stage, including [[follow-pending-input-management-2026-08-19]].

## Decisions

- Field-discovered defects, Pi compatibility, security updates, and evidence-backed UX corrections are maintenance rather than missing product stages.
- User-specific infrastructure and public deployment require separate explicit authorization and never follow implicitly from source work.
- Future redesign or remote-product work begins from fresh product and threat-model evidence rather than reopening this completed ordering list.
